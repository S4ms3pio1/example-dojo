# Welcome to Forensics Dojo

Hello Friend!, this dojo is your first step into the world of digital forensics, the art of finding what's hidden, recovering what's lost, and reading what wasn't meant to be read.

Files lie. A single pixel can hide a secret message. In each challenge, you'll learn to look underneath the surface and uncover what's really there.

No prior experience needed — just a familiarity with basic Linux commands will get you through the challenge.

**The data doesn't lie — if you look hard enough.**

---

Not too long ago, at the time of publishing this dojo. I joined my first online CTF ever. I picked up a few challenges from the Forensics category I figured I'd try something I hadn't done before. I ran `strings`, stared at the screen, and had absolutely no idea what to do.

That curiosity is what drove me here — and to share what I've picked up along the way.

I hope you enjoy it. Good luck, and have fun.
